Boards, badges & building blocks.

Hobby electronics by Michael Cutler, started in 2020. Explore the KiCad sources, schematics and build notes behind Makeristic's boards and tools.

Some projects were manufactured; others remain unfinished designs. Each repository documents its available files, known issues and reuse terms.

Boards & circuits

    USB-C Power Delivery sink with STEMMA QT, screw-terminal output and RGB indication.Work in progress · KiCad source and historical fabrication files

  • STM32WB55 Feather

    A Bluetooth microcontroller board in the Feather form factor, with USB-C and STEMMA QT.Work in progress · Four-layer PCB, BOM and CubeMX configuration

  • STEMMA MachXO2

    A Lattice FPGA breakout with I²C connectors and DebugEdge programming, forked from Chickadee Tech.Historical design · Check package selection and upstream licence terms

    An STM32F042 USB-C current measurement concept with a MAX4239 analogue front end.Unfinished schematic · No PCB layout or firmware

Templates & parts

  • Feather template

    A KiCad starting layout with Micro-USB, battery connector, headers and mounting holes. Derived from arturo182's template.

  • USB-C Feather template

    A USB-C variant of the Feather template. Add the controller, USB-C termination and application circuitry before fabrication.
